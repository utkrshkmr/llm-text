\chapter{References and Further Reading}\label{app:refs}

This appendix collects the works cited across the book: the papers behind the models, algorithms
and datasets, and the systems papers and open-source projects behind the Systems Lens material.
Entries are sorted alphabetically by first author.

\section*{Staying current}
The field moves quickly. Good primary sources include arXiv (cs.CL, cs.LG, cs.DC), the
proceedings of MLSys, OSDI/SOSP, ISCA, NeurIPS, ICML and ICLR, and the documentation and design notes of the
open-source systems discussed in this book: PyTorch, vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM, Megatron-LM,
DeepSpeed, veRL, OpenRLHF, TRL, JAX and Triton.

Every chapter ends with a *Research frontier* section and works through one landmark paper in a
*Reading the research* box; [@sec:landscape:roadmap] describes the parts and gives four reading
paths through them, and [@app:research] turns the habit of reading papers for their cost models
into a method for doing research.
